# Icebox 🧊

Icebox is a mobile app for organizing food in your **fridge, freezer, and pantry**.

It helps you keep track of your food inventory, categories, quantities, and expiration dates. Food information can be retrieved from **Open Food Facts**, including through barcode scanning.

The app also includes an **AI-powered agentic workflow** that lets users manage their inventory through natural language. For example, a user can say *"I bought milk"* and the agent can determine its category, storage location, and estimated shelf life before adding it to the inventory.

## Features

* 📦 Food inventory management
* 🏷️ Categories and storage locations
* 📅 Expiration date tracking
* 📷 Barcode scanning
* 🔎 Open Food Facts integration
* 🤖 AI agent for natural-language inventory management
* 🔄 Multi-step AI tool calling and workflow execution
